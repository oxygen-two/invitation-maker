# Birthday background assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-07 for the approved six-template birthday expansion. Text, dates, names and ages remain editable HTML, not embedded in these images.

Each selected image is converted to 1200 × 900 WebP using the existing cwebp utility and kept below the repository's 80 KiB source-image budget. Original generated PNGs remain outside the repository. No image-generation CLI or new dependency was used.

## silver-afterglow.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: photographic background for sophisticated birthday invitation, NOT a finished card. Create an editorial still-life night-party photograph in silver and pale lilac: polished silver dessert stand holding a small ivory birthday cake with one slender lit candle, a softly folded lilac satin ribbon and a few tiny reflected points of light. Cool metallic sheen, direct-flash fashion-magazine mood with soft lavender shadows, realistic texture and restrained composition. Wide 4:3 landscape composition, objects clustered around center-right with quiet dark lilac space around them so a portrait crop works. No people, no text, no numerals, no logos, no watermark. Avoid balloons, confetti piles, cartoon motifs, wedding symbolism.

A beautifully composed close editorial still life of a peach linen tablecloth, an ivory ceramic plate with a small slice of birthday cake and a single fine lit candle, a clear drinking glass, and softly draped napkin. Warm late-afternoon daylight, realistic fabric and ceramic texture, peach and ivory with pale apricot accents. Quiet modern hospitality magazine feeling, intentional negative space, not excessive decoration. Landscape 4:3 composition suited to center crop into a framed photo. No people, words, letters, ages, logos or watermark. No weddings, no balloons.

Natural window light, subtle shadows, beautiful real petal detail, restrained contemporary floral studio editorial photography.
